Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

AZattle Lab ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by Dhruv Agarwal. It is used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (@PhysicsBattleLab) and Instagram account (@azattlelab). It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's YouTube channel. It uses this only to upload the channel's own videos, set their title, description and thumbnail, and read back their status.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, inside the automation software that performs the uploads. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data from other people

The app does not collect, store or process any personal data of viewers or any other users.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
